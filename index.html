How I work

My Design Process

Every great design starts with the right questions.

01

Research

Understanding users, business goals, and pain points through interviews, surveys, and competitive analysis.

02

Define

Synthesizing research into user personas, journey maps, and problem statements to guide design decisions.

03

Design

Wireframing, prototyping, and building high-fidelity UI in Figma with a focus on visual hierarchy and consistency.

04

Validate

Usability testing, iteration, and refinement based on real user feedback to ensure designs truly solve the problem.
